You need to follow the 3-stage business process.

Based on your answers, your proposal should follow the 3-stafe business case process.

This service is currently being updated and cannot yet identify the exact template you need, but the template you'll need will depend on the stage your proposal has reached. This may be a:

  • project brief
  • strategic outline case (SOC)
  • outline business case (OBC)
  • full business case (FBC)

In some cases, such as minor changes to a previously approved business case, you may not need to complete a new template. Instead, you may be able to use an addendum to update the existing approved business case.

Always use the latest template from the template library. Avoid using a template from a previous business case or one provided by a colleague.

You do not need to return to this service to upload your business case after it has been approved.

What to do next

If you know which template you need:

  • engage with your Subject Matters Experts as early as possible
  • download the template from the template library

If you are unsure which template you need:

  • engage with your Subject Matter Experts as early as possible
  • review the stages guidance on the Project Delivery hub to see which stage your proposal fits in.
  • if you're still unsure, contact the ISC Secretariat at . The ISC Secretariat can help you identify the correct starting template and explain the approval process.
